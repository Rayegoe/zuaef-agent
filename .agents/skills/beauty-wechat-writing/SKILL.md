# Beauty WeChat public-account writing

Platform-stable principles for consumer beauty content. Facts come only from
the ingested material; this skill is method (how to present those facts).

## 1. Consumer benefit first

- Open with the reader's problem or question, grounded in the material's
  topic (e.g. seasonal sensitivity), then introduce the product as one answer.
- Structure toward what the reader actually decides: Is this for my skin?
  What is in it? Does the evidence hold? How much and where?
- Write in plain language: explain ingredient roles in one sentence
  ("神经酰胺像砖墙里的水泥") without turning the piece into a chemistry lesson.

## 2. Present data honestly

- Keep every number exactly as the material gives it (samples, percentages,
  prices, discounts). Rounding a number is a factual change; do it only when
  the material itself rounds it.
- Brand-commissioned or small-sample testing must be labeled as such. If the
  material says "品牌方自报 / 小范围试用", the article says so too — honesty
  about the limits is the trust-building move, not an admission of weakness.
- Include the unflattering rows when the material includes them (participants
  with no change, dropouts). Readers notice the honesty.

## 3. No fabricated testimonials

- Never invent a user review, a "用了三个月" experience, or a scene of
  purchase/use the material does not contain. If the material has a
  product-manager or expert quote, use it as the human voice.
- 不虚构采访现场: do not place a person in a scene the material never
  describes.

## 4. Claims stay inside the material's evidence

- The product's claims (修护屏障、缓解泛红) may be stated as the product's
  claims, attributed to the brand material — not as independently verified
  fact. Mirror the material's hedging.
- If the material lacks an efficacy claim the assignment seems to want, write
  what exists and stop; do not extrapolate.

## 5. Practical clarity

- Include price, size, discount window, and purchase channels when the
  material provides them — these are the details a reader scrolls for.
- End with a concrete, non-pushy takeaway that respects the reader's choice
  (patch-test, consult a doctor for persistent conditions) rather than a
  hard sell.

## 6. When the customer says “太模板化” / “开头有点 AI” (accepted 2026-08-19)

Accepted from learning case `summer-nail-rewrite-20260819` (human-reviewed
ACCEPT). Applied as a natural-language guideline, not a sensor/score rule.

When a draft is structurally correct and constraint-clean but reads like a
公众号模板, the failure is usually the model's 总结欲: every paragraph closes
“material → distillation → judgment sentence”, and the opening leads with an
abstract golden sentence. Fix in this order:

1. Open with a concrete scene anchor first — who, when, where, which object
   (e.g. a customer coming in to remove 水钻 nails). Let the reader see the
   scene before the idea; the judgment lands after, allowed to be quiet.
2. Let the author speak in first person (我们/我) when the format suits the
   brief — a first-person line (“今年我把标准改了：不抢眼就行”) reads as a
   person, not a template.
3. Do not distill every material to the same density. Leave one material
   under-used so the reader feels the shape rather than being told it.
4. Do not open with a judgment/golden sentence; if the brief demands
   conclusion-first (老板要结论前置), keep the conclusion position but anchor
   it with a concrete detail before expanding.

Boundary: this guideline applies to consumer beauty 公众号 writing. It does
NOT apply to formal/report writing where argument density must stay low and
sources come first — do not generalize it to other domains.

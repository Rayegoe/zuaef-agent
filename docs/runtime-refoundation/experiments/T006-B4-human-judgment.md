## Evidence verdict

```text
P = FAIL
Q = FAIL
```

### P failures

1. P says PI published the real-work evaluation. The desk pack says the
   models ran under the PI Harness and separately names the Accio Work team;
   execution framework does not establish publisher attribution.
2. “Not that the Agent is insufficiently intelligent; the surrounding system
   is unready” turns an additional determining factor into an exclusive cause.
3. “A Harness must solve at least five layers” drops the source boundary that
   the industry has no uniform definition and the source is adopting one broad
   analytical definition.

### Q failures

1. It turns one benchmark into “the current state of models in real work”
   without preserving the benchmark scope. The source author's own
   generalization is still an attributed interpretation, not a general fact.
2. It changes “five layers jointly determine whether capability becomes task
   results” into “if any one layer is unresolved, capability cannot become
   business results,” a necessary-condition amplification.
3. Its ending says the Agent will be “responsible for execution results,”
   blurring traceable/verifiable execution with human or organizational
   responsibility after correctly distinguishing them earlier.

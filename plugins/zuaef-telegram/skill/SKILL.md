# Telegram Reporting

Use `report_to_telegram` to notify the configured human chat of material
milestones, completion, blocking failures, or when human attention is
required.

Rules:

- Report completion, material milestones, blocking failures, and decisions
  that need human attention.
- Keep reports concise and outcome-oriented (one or two sentences).
- Do not report routine internal reasoning or every tool action.
- Never send secrets or private source material unless explicitly authorized.

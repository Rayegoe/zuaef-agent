# 13 — Live Forward Evidence

Live forward is the strongest evidence because it is frozen before the outcome is known.

Use current settlement semantics:
D+1 / D+3 / D+5 / D+8 + MFE/MAE.

Milestones:
- 5–10 trading days: operational continuity;
- 20–30 valid settled triggers: preliminary signal evidence;
- 50–100 triggers: early meaningful assessment;
- 100+ across different regimes: stronger evidence.

First formal M1 audit:
20 trading days OR 30 settled valid forward triggers, whichever comes first,
with zero unresolved evidence-pipeline integrity failures.

If a healthy candidate pool produces zero triggers for 10–15 trading days:
diagnose in replay/sandbox; never loosen production just to manufacture activity.

Track executed vs skipped opportunities separately.
